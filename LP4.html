About SWMP

National Excellence in Medical Logistics & Storage

Strategic Warehouse & Medical Products (SWMP) is the premier national pharmaceutical logistics organization affiliated with the Unified Procurement Authority (UPA). We operate state-of-the-art facilities designed specifically for receiving, inspecting, storing, and managing medicines, vaccines, and medical devices.

Through our seamless SAP S/4HANA integration, we provide end-to-end digital visibility, regulatory compliance, and unmatched operational speed for pharmaceutical manufacturers, importers, hospitals, and global healthcare distributors.

Support & Info

Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers regarding onboarding, portal usage, and warehousing procedures.

How do I become a registered customer?
You can begin by submitting our contact form or reaching out directly. Our onboarding team will schedule a facility walkthrough, review your commercial documents, and assist with initial contract signing.
How do I receive portal login credentials?
Once your storage agreement is finalized and verified, your designated corporate administrator will receive secure temporary login credentials via encrypted email.
How do I submit a new storage request?
Log into the Customer Portal, navigate to 'Storage Requests', click 'New Request', choose your required temperature category, fill in product batch details, and submit for instant SAP processing.
Can I track my inventory in real-time?
Yes. The portal provides live inventory visibility displaying active stock levels, batch numbers, expiry dates, and exact warehouse location mapping.
How do I request product release for hospital delivery?
Simply go to 'Release Requests' in the portal, select the items and quantities you wish to dispatch, specify the destination, and submit the order for fulfillment.
