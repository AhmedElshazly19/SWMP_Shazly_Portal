About SWMP

SWMP is a national pharmaceutical warehousing and logistics organization, ensuring the safe storage and distribution of medicines, medical devices, and healthcare supplies.

🎯 Core Competency

Fully validated, GMP/GDP-compliant 3PL partner specializing in temperature-sensitive pharmaceutical storage and supply chain execution.

Infrastructure

🏗️ Validated Storage Zones

Our facility is engineered to accommodate diverse pharmaceutical storage profiles with precision and reliability.

Cold Storage (Cold Chain)

2°C to 8°C

Vaccines, biologics, insulins, and sensitive APIs requiring strict temperature control.

Controlled Room Temperature

15°C to 25°C

Dynamic humidity control (RH ≤ 60%) for stable pharmaceutical products.

Freezer Storage

−25°C to −15°C

Deep-freeze for plasma, biological materials, and temperature-critical substances.

Secure Quarantine Cages

Physically & Electronically Locked

Incoming inspection, returned goods, and rejected stock with full access control.

Monitoring

📊 Environmental Monitoring

Thermal Mapping

Every storage zone is fully mapped across seasonal extremes using high-density 3D sensor grids to eliminate hot and cold spots.

24/7 Continuous Monitoring

Real-time temperature and humidity tracking with automated backups and UPS power redundancy.

Multi-Tiered Alarms

Automated SMS, email, and phone escalations triggered immediately if parameters drift outside acceptable limits.
