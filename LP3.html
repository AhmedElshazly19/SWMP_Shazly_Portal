About SWMP

SWMP is a national pharmaceutical warehousing and logistics organization, affiliated with UPA. We ensure the safe storage and distribution of medicines, medical devices, and healthcare supplies.

Mission: To provide secure, temperature-controlled storage with full traceability.

Vision: Digital transformation of pharmaceutical logistics through SAP integration.

Our partnership with UPA and SAP enables real-time inventory visibility, GDP/GMP compliance, and 24/7 monitoring.

FAQ

How do I become a customer?
Contact our onboarding team via the contact form or call us. We'll guide you through the contract and portal setup.
How do I receive portal credentials?
After signing the contract, you'll receive an email with your username and temporary password.
How do I submit a request?
Log in to the portal, click "New Storage Request", fill in product details and submit.
How do I track products?
Use the "Inventory" section to search by batch, name, or location.
